Niacinamide

Niacinamide is a water-soluble vitamin that works with the natural substances in your skin to help visibly improve enlarged pores, uneven skin tone, fine lines and wrinkles, dullness, and a weakened surface.

verified How it works

It helps build cells in the skin while also protecting them from environmental stresses, such as sunlight, pollution, and toxins. It acts as a precursor to essential coenzymes for many cellular metabolic enzymic reactions.

spa Benefits

  • Minimizes pore appearance
  • Regulates oil production
  • Treats hyperpigmentation
  • Protects against oxidative stress

Detailed Guide

Beginner's Protocol

Start with a 5% concentration if you have sensitive skin. While 10% is popular, clinical studies show 5% is highly effective for most concerns without irritation risk.

add_circle BEST WITH

  • Hyaluronic Acid: Boosts hydration
  • Retinol: Mitigates irritation
  • Zinc: Superior oil control

block AVOID MIXING

Avoid mixing in the same step with L-Ascorbic Acid (Pure Vit C) if it causes flushing, though modern stabilized versions are usually fine.

warning

Safety Notes & Side Effects

Rare but possible: mild redness or "niacin flushing" when used with highly acidic products. Always patch test on the forearm for 24 hours before facial application.
